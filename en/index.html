Features

Everything about your spend at a glance

The extension repeats only the requests the DeepSeek web console makes and turns them into a readable dashboard.

Wallet balance

Paid and bonus balance shown separately. The badge turns red once your balance drops below the threshold you set.

Spend today

Today's spend and token count. “Today” is fetched on its own so the number is accurate until the day rolls over.

Per-key accounting

See which key costs the most. The badge and the chart can be scoped to a single key or any selection of keys.

30-day chart

Daily bars for the selected window. Click keys in the list and the bars become stacked segments with a colour legend.

Badge on your metric

Spend today, spend in the period, balance or tokens — you decide what the toolbar icon shows.

Auto refresh

Data refreshes in the background on a schedule (once a minute by default) or not at all. A manual refresh button is always there.

Budgets

FAQ

Frequently asked

Do I need a DeepSeek API key?

No. The extension uses the session already open in your browser on platform.deepseek.com. No API key or password is requested or stored.

Does the extension send my data anywhere else?

No. Requests go only to platform.deepseek.com; everything else stays local. No analytics, telemetry or ads.

The badge shows “−”, what should I do?

The DeepSeek WAF token expired. Open platform.deepseek.com once — the token renews itself and the extension picks it up on the next refresh. The extension never opens tabs on its own.

The badge shows “!” or an authorization error

The session was not found or has expired. Sign in to the platform, then press “Re-detect session” in the extension settings.

How accurate is the “today” figure?

The platform's daily series does not include the current day until it ends, so the extension takes “today” from the hourly series and adds it to the chart separately.

Is there a Firefox version?

Chrome and Chromium-based browsers only (Edge, Brave, Arc) — Chrome 116+ is required.
